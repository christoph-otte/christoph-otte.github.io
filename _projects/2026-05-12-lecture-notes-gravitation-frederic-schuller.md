In the summer term 2014, Frederic P. Schuller gave a [lecture on classical mechanics](https://www.fau.tv/series/theoretische-physik-1-mechanik-tp-1-s14) at University of Erlangen-Nuremberg. In his lecture, Prof Schuller combined elements of differential topology with applications in Newtonian, Lagrangian, and Hamiltonian mechanics.

You can find the source code and the [PDF](https://github.com/christoph-otte/klassische-mechanik/blob/stable/main.pdf) on [GitHub](https://github.com/christoph-otte/klassische-mechanik). Please note that the lecture was delivered in German.
