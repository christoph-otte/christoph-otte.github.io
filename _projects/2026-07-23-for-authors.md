## Who Can Submit?

Any student currently enrolled at a university or other institution of higher education is welcome to submit. This includes bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students. Co-authorship with fellow students is encouraged; faculty co-authorship is permitted but the primary author must be a student.

## What Can Be Submitted?

We publish:

- **Original research articles** — new data, experiments, or simulations
- **Review articles** — structured overviews of a field or topic
- **Short communications** — brief reports of focused, self-contained findings
- **Theoretical / computational articles** — analytical or numerical work without new experimental data

We welcome work from all natural science disciplines including (but not limited to) physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, earth sciences, and computational science.

---

## Editorial Philosophy

We are not looking for perfect papers — we are looking for honest, rigorous, and clearly communicated science. Our reviewers are trained to give constructive feedback, not just accept or reject. If your work has a clear question and a careful answer, it belongs here.
